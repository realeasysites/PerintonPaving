The difference

You can see it in the edges.

Anyone can dump blacktop. What separates a top-tier driveway is the detail work: a solid base, even compaction, and edges that are hand-tamped crisp and clean, not left ragged into the lawn. That's the standard on every Perinton job, and it's why builders trust us with the drives on their best homes.

Who we are

We are Integrity | Value | Pride | Paving

Perinton Paving has been laying blacktop out of Fairport since 1968. Over the decades that has meant a lot of driveways, a lot of early mornings, and a reputation as one of the highest-quality residential paving outfits in the Rochester area.

The formula hasn't changed: experienced people, top-notch equipment and high-quality materials. And after all these years, the owner is still on the scene, on the job site making sure every driveway is done with integrity.

We give you a straight, free estimate, we schedule around your needs, and we stand behind the finished job with a one-year guarantee on materials and workmanship.
